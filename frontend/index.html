A binary test asks "are you a leader or not." A real person is 70% leader under pressure and 40% on a slow Tuesday. Forcing that into a box throws away the truth.

02 / The method

Mamdani inference, not multiple choiceEvery trait, blended,
not boxed.

70 questions score 20 traits from 0–10. A fuzzy engine — the same class of logic used in real control systems — lets a trait be partly "high" and partly "very high" at once, then ranks your fit against 105 careers.

Typical quiz

CareerLens
