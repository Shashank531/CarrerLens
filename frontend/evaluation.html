Evaluation Methodology & Mathematical Formulations

Career recommendation is formulated as an Information Retrieval (IR) ranking and multi-attribute decision problem. Evaluating recommendations measures top-choice precision alongside top-k exploration utility across 105 careers.

Top-1 Exact Match
Top-1 Acc = (Correct Top-1 / N) × 100

Measures the percentage of test cases where the system's single highest-ranked career exactly matches the student's expected target role.

Top-5 Hit Rate
Top-5 Hit Rate = (Expected ∈ Top-5 / N) × 100

Standard metric for recommender systems. Reflects whether the target career is captured within the student's primary recommendation dashboard.

Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR)
MRR = (1/N) × Σ (1 / Rank_i)

Quantifies ranking quality. A score of 1.0 indicates all targets at Rank 1. Rank 2 contributes 0.50, Rank 3 contributes 0.33, Rank 5 contributes 0.20.
